About the project

One thoughtful place to plan a trip.

Why TravelMate AI?

TravelMate AI is a college hackathon platform that reduces fragmented trip research. It combines destination discovery, maps, a local mock AI planner, budget calculation, safety reminders, and culture-first recommendations in one responsive Django app.

Mission

Make Indian travel more accessible, safe and sustainable.

Stack

Django, SQLite, vanilla JS, Bootstrap and Leaflet.

Ready to grow

Service layer keeps API keys off the frontend.
